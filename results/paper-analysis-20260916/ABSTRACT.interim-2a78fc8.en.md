# Abstract

Large language model agents depend on external feedback to test hypotheses, retrieve information, and verify conclusions. EXPGYM studies how feedback budgets alter observed performance, model selection, and coordination across tuning, multi-hop search, and evidence audit. After a full-cohort answer-extraction and voting repair, tightening feedback from Free to Tight reduces search F1 for 6 of six models and exact evidence-set accuracy for 6 of six. The highest observed model changes in 5 of seven evaluation dimensions. Free-based model selection can incur nontrivial Tight regret, reaching 15.22 Gap points on hpobench:paramnet:adult:steps. Complete behavioral analysis covers 486 tuning, 1,314 search, and 702 audit trajectories and distinguishes attempted calls, visible feedback, stopping, submitted configurations, and fallback scores. For hypotheses requiring nonempty evidence, label accuracy changes from 85.86% to 83.42%, while exact evidence-set accuracy changes from 81.61% to 45.05%. With four agents and matched per-agent feedback budgets, POOLACT's median Tight-search gain over independent rollouts is 7.01 F1 percentage points; audit evidence accuracy exceeds both independent and cache-sharing baselines in 12 of twelve model-budget settings. Audit trajectories associate these gains with broader verification coverage. These results are descriptive, preserve negative comparisons, and do not establish universal model superiority or isolate individual coordination mechanisms.

This interim report uses the completed rescoring of all 4,698 existing main-experiment slots. The 97 registered HPO multi-agent control reruns have not yet been incorporated; HPO coordination estimates remain explicitly labeled as historical executions rescored under the repaired rules. Historical published scores, earlier diagnostics, and the new scoring version remain separate. Gemini combines historical Sub2 and OpenRouter backfills. HPO runtime graph-key collisions cannot be repaired by changing stored scores: version-specific historical runs and uniform repaired controls are tracked separately. Model/provider differences, thinking/output settings, physical scheduling, and the fixed Audit prompt example remain interpretation limits; matched feedback budgets do not imply matched computational expenditure.
